4. Additional Notes
The game is a small top-down dodge trainer inspired by League of Legends"Dodge Show".
The player controls a small Yasuo ("Suor") and:
(1)Right-clicks to move.
(2)Presses E to dash with a cooldown.
(3)Gains a passive shield when MP is full.
(4)Scores +1 point per second while alive.
